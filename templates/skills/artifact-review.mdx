---
title: "Artifact Review"
description: "Review a derived artifact for completeness before approval."
triggers: [artifact review]
---
When asked to review a derived artifact, check that it is complete for its
kind and that it derives from accepted content:

- Requirements: one behaviour, one id, one acceptance criterion.
- Decisions: a decision and its rationale; no restated requirements.
- Open questions: a question with the context that raised it.
- Documentation: scope, storage notes, and acceptance surfaces.

Report gaps as a list. Do not edit the artifact.
